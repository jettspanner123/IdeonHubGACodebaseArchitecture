# Admin-provisioned accounts with per-app, per-role entitlements

IdeonHub has no public self-registration — every account is created by an `IDEON_HUB_SUPER_ADMIN`, a role independent of any Client Application's own roles, through one central admin panel rather than delegated per-app admins. Having an account never implies access to any app: access is a separate, explicit `(user, app, role)` entitlement, and each app declares its own valid role names to IdeonHub so the panel can only assign real roles, never free text. We chose this over self-registration (even with company-email-domain gating) because letting people self-declare their own identity is a weaker starting posture for a system whose entire job is "this person works here and is who they say they are," and over a single global role because the four apps' role vocabularies don't overlap at all (SignForge has no `OPERATOR`, AssetSphere has no `HR_MANAGER`) — a global role can't honestly represent what "admin" means differently in each one. The trade-off is a support bottleneck risk (every new hire or app-access request routes through a small group of super-admins) and more upfront data modeling (entitlements as their own table, not a flag on the user) than a simpler "one account, access everywhere" design would need.
